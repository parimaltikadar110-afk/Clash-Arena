About ClashX7

ClashX7 is an online tournament platform for players interested in competitive custom-room gaming.

What We Offer

Users can explore available tournaments, review match rules, check match details and follow results through the platform.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide users with clear tournament information and an accessible gaming experience.

Support

For questions or assistance, use the customer support contact available in the ClashX7 app.

ClashX7 is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena or Free Fire.
